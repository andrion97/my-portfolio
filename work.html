Concept case study

AI meeting copilot for sales teams

Turning messy sales calls into clean CRM updates and next steps — automatically.

Problem
Reps spend hours after calls writing notes and updating the CRM, so data is late, thin, or missing.
Approach
An LLM listens to the call, drafts a summary, flags risks and next steps, and suggests CRM field updates the rep approves in one click.
How I'd measure it
Time spent on post-call admin, CRM field completeness, and weekly active reps.

Concept case study

Cutting time-to-value in B2B onboarding

Getting new customers to their first "aha" moment in days instead of weeks.

Problem
New accounts stall during setup, and many never reach the feature that makes them stick.
Approach
Map the activation funnel, define one clear activation event, then replace the generic tour with a role-based setup checklist and in-product nudges.
How I'd measure it
Activation rate, median time-to-activation, and 90-day retention by cohort.

  • Funnel analysis
  • Experimentation
Concept case study

Churn early-warning for account managers

Spotting at-risk customers before they go quiet — not after they cancel.

Problem
Account managers find out about churn risk too late, usually at renewal time.
Approach
Combine usage drops, support tickets, and stakeholder changes into a simple health score, with a weekly "who needs a call" list and suggested plays.
How I'd measure it
Gross revenue retention, share of at-risk accounts contacted within a week, and score precision.
  • Predictive models
  • Customer success
  • Dashboards
Concept case study

Self-serve support assistant

An AI assistant that answers from the help docs — and knows when to hand off to a human.

Problem
Support queues fill up with repeat questions that are already answered in the docs.
Approach
Retrieval-augmented chat grounded in the knowledge base, with cited answers, a confidence threshold, and smooth escalation to an agent with full context.
How I'd measure it
Deflection rate, resolution satisfaction, escalation accuracy, and answer-hallucination rate from spot checks.
  • RAG
  • AI safety
  • Support ops
